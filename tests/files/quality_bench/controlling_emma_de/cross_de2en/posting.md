Senior Controller (m/f/d) — Arnold Antriebstechnik GmbH
Stuttgart-Vaihingen · Full-time · Permanent

About us
Arnold Antriebstechnik develops and manufactures electric drive systems for industrial
applications. 650 employees, three companies in Germany and Austria, a growing service
business.

Your responsibilities
- Independent preparation of monthly and annual financial reporting (HGB, German GAAP)
  for the group of companies.
- Building up and further developing group consolidation (three companies).
- Expanding our management reporting with Power BI (self-service dashboards for Sales,
  Production and executive management).
- Budgeting process and rolling forecast at group level.
- Production and project controlling for plant engineering (POC valuation,
  percentage-of-completion).
- Supporting the upcoming switch of selected reporting components to IFRS.
- Functional (professional) leadership of a small controlling team (2 people) with a
  development path towards team lead.
- Sparring partner to executive management on investment and make-or-buy decisions.

Your profile
- Completed university degree in business administration or economics.
- At least 5 years of controlling experience in an industrial company.
- Very good knowledge of HGB financial statements (German GAAP) and cost accounting.
- Confident use of SAP CO/FI.
- Experience with consolidation (e.g. LucaNet or similar) desirable.
- Hands-on experience with Power BI or a comparable BI tool.
- Basic knowledge of IFRS an advantage.
- First leadership experience desirable.
- Knowledge of US GAAP an advantage (US customer projects).
- Very good German and good English.
