Staff Backend Engineer — Payments Platform (m/f/d)
NovaPay GmbH · Frankfurt am Main (hybrid, 2 days on-site) · Full-time
Salary band: €115,000–140,000

About NovaPay
NovaPay operates a payments platform for European e-commerce merchants: checkout,
settlement, and payout rails processing millions of transactions per month. We are a
PSD2-licensed payment institution supervised by BaFin. Engineering is organised in four
product teams (about 30 engineers) around the settlement domain.

Your role
You set the technical direction of our settlement and payout domain across all four teams.
You own its architecture, raise the engineering bar for senior engineers and tech leads, and
remain hands-on on the hardest production problems. You report to the Head of Engineering and
work with Engineering Managers, product, SRE and compliance.

What you will do
- Define and own the target architecture of the settlement domain across four teams; write
  RFCs/ADRs and drive them to adoption without line authority.
- Lead cross-team technical initiatives end to end, e.g. exactly-once settlement events on
  Apache Kafka and the standards for our EKS platform.
- Set reliability standards across teams: SLOs and error budgets, incident-review culture,
  on-call design.
- Mentor senior engineers and tech leads; help calibrate engineering levels and take part in
  hiring decisions and interview loops.
- Translate regulatory requirements (PSD2, auditability) into technical design together with
  compliance and BaFin-facing leadership.
- Stay hands-on: write and review critical code and lead the response to major incidents.

What you bring
- 8+ years of professional backend engineering, deep Python expertise (FastAPI or Django).
- A track record of leading architecture decisions that span multiple teams or services,
  documented and adopted beyond your own team.
- Expert PostgreSQL experience for financial data (auditability, idempotency, performance
  on large tables).
- Production experience with Apache Kafka at scale (exactly-once or idempotent processing).
- Led Kubernetes adoption or platform work in production (EKS, GitOps, IaC).
- Observability and SLO practice that you have rolled out across several teams.
- Demonstrated mentoring of senior engineers or tech leads, not only mid-level colleagues.
- Payments domain experience; PSD2 and regulated-environment (BaFin, PCI DSS) experience
  is expected.
- Security-minded engineering for regulated financial data.
- Clear written and spoken communication with executives and non-technical stakeholders.
- German C1 and English C1.

Nice to have
- Experience with crypto/blockchain settlement rails.
