Senior Backend Engineer — Payments Platform (m/f/d)
NovaPay GmbH · Frankfurt am Main (hybrid, 2 days on-site) · Full-time
Salary band: €85,000–105,000

About NovaPay
NovaPay operates a payments platform for European e-commerce merchants: checkout,
settlement, and payout rails processing millions of transactions per month. We are a
PSD2-licensed payment institution supervised by BaFin.

Your role
You will own backend services at the heart of our settlement pipeline — high-volume,
correctness-critical, and observable by design. You work in a cross-functional team
with product, SRE and compliance.

What you will do
- Design, build and operate Python backend services for settlement and payout flows.
- Evolve our event-driven architecture on Apache Kafka (exactly-once settlement events).
- Run your services on Kubernetes (EKS) with infrastructure-as-code.
- Model and tune PostgreSQL schemas for financial data (auditability, idempotency).
- Build the observability for what you ship: Prometheus metrics, Grafana dashboards, SLOs.
- Review designs and mentor engineers across the payments group.

What you bring
- 5+ years of professional backend engineering with Python (FastAPI or Django).
- Solid PostgreSQL experience including performance work on large tables.
- Production experience with Apache Kafka or comparable event streaming.
- Hands-on Kubernetes experience in production.
- AWS experience (we run on EKS, RDS, S3).
- CI/CD ownership and a testing culture you can articulate.
- Observability practice: Prometheus/Grafana or equivalent, SLO thinking.
- Interest in the payments domain; PSD2/PSP integration experience is a strong plus.
- Security-minded engineering (we handle regulated financial data).
- Mentoring experience and clear technical communication.
- German C1 and English B2 or better.

Nice to have
- Experience with crypto/blockchain settlement rails.
- BaFin/regulated-environment exposure.

We offer
Hybrid work, a learning budget, and a settlement pipeline you can be proud of.
