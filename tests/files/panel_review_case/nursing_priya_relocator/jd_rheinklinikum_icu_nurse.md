Registered Nurse — Intensive Care Unit (m/f/d)
Rhein Klinikum Bonn · International Recruitment Programme · Full-time, shift work

About us
Rhein Klinikum Bonn is a 900-bed academic teaching hospital. Our interdisciplinary
ICU (32 beds, levels 2–3) cares for ventilated and post-surgical patients. Our
international programme has welcomed nurses from 14 countries; we provide structured
onboarding in English while you complete your German recognition.

Your role
- Bedside intensive care for ventilated and haemodynamically unstable adult patients.
- Invasive monitoring, vasoactive therapy, renal replacement therapy (CRRT).
- Structured handovers (SBAR) and electronic documentation.
- Participation in the unit's emergency response and resuscitation team.
- Mentoring of trainees once established.

Your profile
- Nursing degree and an active nursing registration (EU or non-EU).
- At least 3 years of adult ICU experience.
- Confident with mechanical ventilation and invasive monitoring.
- CRRT experience desirable.
- Recognition of your qualification in Germany (Anerkennung) completed or in
  progress — we support the process (Anpassungslehrgang possible in-house).
- German level B1 with commitment to reach B2 within 12 months (language courses
  paid); B2 preferred. Our documentation transitions to German after onboarding.
- ALS or equivalent resuscitation certification.
- Experience with electronic patient records.
- Team spirit, resilience, and willingness to work a 3-shift rota.

Desirable
- ECMO experience.
- Paediatric intensive care experience.
- Wound-management qualification.

We offer
Relocation support (visa, housing for the first 6 months), recognition coaching,
paid language courses, TVöD-based pay.
